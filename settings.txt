SETTINGS

1. Account Info
- Display Name: display name + edit button
- Username: @username + edit button
- Phone Number: status + add button
- Email: masked email + verified badge + edit button
- Password: hidden field + edit button
- Age Group: badge (13-15 Checked)
- Birthday: date + verified badge
- Gender: Male / Female selector
- Language: dropdown

2. Privacy & Content Restrictions
- Content restrictions:
  * Content maturity: Minimal, Mild, Moderate
  * Blocked games: list
  * Allowed games: list
  * Preview games: selector
- Communication:
  * Chat and party with friends: On / Off
  * Camera Input: On / Off
  * Live collaboration in Studio: On / Off
- Visibility & private servers:
  * Visibility:
    + Online status: Everyone, Friends & followers, Friends, Trusted friends, No one
    + Show current game: Everyone, Friends & followers, Friends, Trusted friends, No one
    + Share activity updates: On / Off
  * Private servers: Friends, Everyone, No one
- Friends & contacts: search & sync
- Trading & inventory: All, Friends, No one
- Blocked users: list (Display Name, @Username, Unblock button)
